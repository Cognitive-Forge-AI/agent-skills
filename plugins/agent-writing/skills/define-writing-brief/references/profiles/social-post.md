# Social post

Select one useful point and enough context for a reader seeing the post alone. Adapt the supplied material to the requested platform and audience. Honour an explicit character or word limit; when none is given, prefer a compact post without inventing a platform limit.

Retain qualifications that change the claim. Compression does not justify converting a possibility into a promise, a test into a broad outcome, or an unmeasured benefit into a statistic. Preserve source attribution when it matters to understanding or confidence. If a required claim cannot fit with its qualification, propose a shorter supported claim or surface the constraint.

Use a thread only when requested or needed for the scope. Do not add hashtags, emojis, promotional hooks, engagement questions, or calls to action by default. Follow the author's voice and platform-supported presentation; plain text is a reasonable fallback.
