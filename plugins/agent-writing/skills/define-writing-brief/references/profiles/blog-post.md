# Blog post

Make a standalone piece useful to its intended audience. Establish the subject and relevant context early enough for readers arriving without earlier posts. Give the piece a clear purpose and enough supported detail to sustain it. Use the selected structure for progression and the selected style for voice.

Choose length, headings, and examples from the brief and the complexity of the point. An argument may lead with a claim; a reflective post may earn its point through a specific experience supplied by the author. Neither requires a promotional hook or call to action.

Keep dates, measurements, experiences, and outcomes traceable to the source. Label inference and uncertainty. Link to available supporting material where it helps readers assess a claim. A blog is a medium, so its Markdown, HTML, or other output syntax comes from the presentation selection.
