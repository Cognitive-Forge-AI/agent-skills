# Community guidelines

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss ideas and code constructively, explain disagreements, and make space for different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, repeated personal attacks, and publishing someone else's private information without permission are unacceptable. These guidelines apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to [opensource@cognitiveforge.ai](mailto:opensource@cognitiveforge.ai). Include enough context for maintainers to investigate and avoid sharing unnecessary personal information. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly where practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. They will consider context and explain moderation decisions when appropriate.
